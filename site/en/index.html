Learning Astrology on Your Own

A step-by-step path from the basics to real practice: planets, zodiac signs, houses, aspects — and drawing up your first natal charts.

Build your natal chart →

You can learn astrology on your own: all the core material is freely available, and free online programs calculate charts for you, with no need to know the maths or read an ephemeris. What matters most is studying things in the right order — and this site is built around exactly that plan.

Study plan

  1. Learn all the planets

    The Sun and Moon (the luminaries), Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto, plus Chiron, Proserpina, the karmic lunar nodes, and Black Moon Lilith and the White Moon (fictitious points). The goal is to grasp each planet's essential nature — what it is at its core, regardless of where it falls in a chart.

  2. The zodiac signs and their rulers

    Go back over the twelve signs in more depth and understand how they connect to their ruling planets: why Mars rules Aries, and Venus rules both Taurus and Libra.

  3. The houses — the most important part

    The twelve houses, understood by analogy with the signs. Houses show which areas of life the planets' energy plays out in: family, career, partnership, health…

  4. Aspects between the planets

    Trines, sextiles, squares and oppositions — and conjunctions matter a great deal too. Aspects are the planets "talking" to one another.

  5. Practice

    Practise on the people around you: friends, colleagues and relatives. Charts can be calculated online for free — for example, with Sotis-Online.

Audio lectures

The site also hosts recorded lectures — informal explanations from a practising astrologer. Listen to them alongside the reading material.

Do you need a course?

Everything you need is freely available, but you can take a course if you'd like. Konstantin Daragan's Academy of Astrology in St. Petersburg, for example, offers structured training — useful, but not essential: following this plan on your own is enough for a confident start.
