Step 2. The Zodiac Signs and Their Rulers

A sign is the “stage” on which a planet performs: it colors the planet’s expression with its own character. The key to understanding the signs is their connection to their ruling planets.

If the planet is what is acting, the sign is how it acts. Mars in Aries acts directly and instantly; Mars in Libra acts through negotiation and partnership. Each sign is ruled by a planet akin to it in nature: the sign is like a “home” in which that planet feels it is the master of the house.

Elements and Modalities

The twelve signs are sorted by two independent divisions:

  • Four elements — the sign’s temperament: Fire (energy, impulse), Earth (practicality, form), Air (thought, connections), Water (feeling, depth).
  • Three modalities — the way it acts: cardinal (initiates), fixed (holds and accumulates), mutable (adapts and completes).

Each combination of element and modality defines exactly one sign: Fire + cardinal = Aries, Fire + fixed = Leo, and so on.

How a Sign and Its Ruler Connect

Notice that the nature of a sign is the nature of its ruler, set against the “scenery” of its element and modality:

  • Mars (action, drive) rules Aries — the most direct and combative sign.
  • Venus (love, values) rules both sensual Taurus (Venus’s earthy side — the body, resources) and refined Libra (her airy side — relationships, aesthetics).
  • Mercury (the mind) rules Gemini (gathering information) and Virgo (analyzing it and putting it to use).
  • Saturn (structure) rules Capricorn; before Uranus was discovered, it ruled Aquarius as well.

A planet in its “own” sign (in its domicile) is strong and acts cleanly. In the opposite sign (in detriment) it struggles: Mars in Libra has to fight with diplomacy. There are also signs of exaltation (the planet is a guest at a friend’s house and shines brightly) and fall (its expression is weak). These dignities and debilities of the planets are an important part of chart analysis.

Exercise: take any sign and try to derive its qualities from its three components — element, modality and ruler. For example, Scorpio: Water (feeling) + fixed (holding on, concentration) + Pluto (transformation) = deep, concentrated, transformative emotions.
