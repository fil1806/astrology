Step 1. The Planets

The first and most fundamental step is to understand the essential nature of each planet: what it means in and of itself, before it ever lands in a sign, a house or an aspect.

In astrology, the word “planets” covers the luminaries (the Sun and the Moon), the true planets, and fictitious (calculated) points alike. Each planet represents a particular kind of energy and a particular function of the psyche.

The Luminaries

☉

Sun

The core of the personality: spirit, will, creative self-expression, vitality. It shows “who I am” and where life is headed. In a man’s chart it is the man himself; in a woman’s chart it also stands for the important men in her life (father, husband).

will · identity · creativity · vitality
☽

Moon

The soul, emotions, the subconscious, habits, adaptation, the need for safety. It shows how a person feels and how they care for others. Linked to the mother, home, childhood and nourishment.

emotions · care · habits · home

Personal Planets

☿

Mercury

Mind, thinking, speech, learning, the exchange of information, trade, short trips. How a person thinks and talks.

intellect · speech · connections · learning
♀

Venus

Love, beauty, harmony, values, money, pleasure, a sense of taste. What a person loves and how they show affection.

love · beauty · values · harmony
♂

Mars

Action, energy, drive, struggle, courage, sexuality, competition. How a person acts and stands up for themselves.

action · will to win · aggression · initiative

Social Planets

♃

Jupiter

Expansion, growth, luck, worldview, higher education, long-distance travel, social success, generosity. The “Greater Benefic” of traditional astrology.

expansion · luck · philosophy · authority
♄

Saturn

Limitation, structure, discipline, time, responsibility, karma, maturity. A demanding teacher who also delivers lasting results. The “Greater Malefic” — until its lessons have been learned.

discipline · boundaries · time · responsibility

Outer Planets

Slow-moving planets, invisible to the naked eye. They work on the level of whole generations, but in a personal chart they come through strongly when they sit on the angles or aspect the personal planets.

♅

Uranus

Freedom, revolution, the sudden and unexpected, invention, originality, astrology, technology and electricity. It breaks down the old to make way for the new.

freedom · suddenness · genius · reform
♆

Neptune

Mysticism, dreams, inspiration, compassion, music, secrets, illusion and deception, the dissolving of boundaries. Higher love — and fog.

mysticism · inspiration · illusion · compassion
♇

Pluto

Transformation, power, death and rebirth, the masses, crises, depth psychology. It tears things down to the foundations — in order to rebuild them.

transformation · power · crisis · rebirth

Chiron and Proserpina

⚷

Chiron

The “wounded healer”: the place where we are vulnerable, yet precisely through which we learn to heal others. Also mediation, unconventional solutions, mentoring.

healing · vulnerability · mentoring
⯓

Proserpina

A hypothetical planet used by some schools. Deep restructuring by breaking something down to its elements and putting it back together: analysis, service, total change with no way back.

restructuring · analysis · irreversible change

Fictitious Points

These are not physical bodies but calculated points on orbits, yet in chart interpretation they work just like planets.

☊

North Node

The karmic task of this lifetime: the direction of growth, the new qualities you need to develop. Heading that way is hard, but that is where development lies.

growth goal · the future · a new program
☋

South Node

Experience accumulated in the past, familiar patterns of behavior. A source of support — but also a trap if you stay there and nowhere else.

past experience · the familiar · exit point
⚸

Black Moon Lilith

The apogee of the Moon’s orbit. The shadow side of the psyche: temptations, fears, accumulated distortions through which a person is tested.

shadow · temptation · karmic distortions
⯝

White Moon (Selena)

The point of bright karma: accumulated good, protection, ease and help in the areas of life marked by its position.

bright karma · protection · grace
Tip: don’t memorize lists of keywords by rote. Picture each planet as a living character — Mars the warrior, Saturn the teacher, Uranus the rebel — and the “personality” will stick on its own.
